Our Professional Services

Targeted solutions designed to eliminate revenue leakage and secure correct payer reimbursement.

90+ Day Accounts Receivable Recovery

Aged accounts sitting past 90 days are prone to write-offs due to timely filing limits or unaddressed payer friction. We deploy systematic follow-up workflows, manage appeals, and directly escalate technical denials to recover trapped revenue for your clinic.

Surgical Claims & Medical Record Auditing

Complex claims require thorough clinical review. We execute deep-dive root cause audits of lengthy hospital notes and operative reports to spot coding discrepancies, ensure documentation alignment, and protect your practice from future compliance audits.
