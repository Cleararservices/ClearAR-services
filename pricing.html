2. Pricing & Contingency Model

11. What does contingency-based pricing mean?

It means our compensation is a percentage of the actual revenue we successfully recover for your practice. If nothing is recovered, you owe nothing.

12. Are there monthly retainer fees?

Our standard contingency model has zero monthly retainers, eliminating financial risk for your practice.

13. Can we choose a flat-fee or hourly structure instead?

Yes, we adapt our pricing based on client preference, including project-based or hourly auditing rates if requested.

14. How is the contingency percentage calculated?

It is based strictly on the total dollars successfully collected and deposited from aged or denied accounts.

15. When are contingency fees invoiced?

Fees are invoiced on an agreed periodic schedule (such as monthly) based on the collections verified during that period.

16. Are there any hidden administrative charges?

No. All terms and fee structures are fully agreed upon in writing before any work commences.

17. What happens if a claim cannot be recovered?

If an aged claim proves uncollectible due to hard filing limits, you pay zero fees for that account.

18. Do you offer discounted rates for high-volume backlogs?

Yes, fee structures can be customized for extensive legacy AR backlogs exceeding standard thresholds.

19. How do client preferences influence contract terms?

We prioritize flexibility and tailor our engagement scope to fit your internal cash-flow goals.

20. Is contract cancellation flexible?

Yes, our agreements outline clear, straightforward terms for conclusion or cancellation upon project completion.

3. AR Recovery & Denials Management

21. What constitutes an aged AR account?

Accounts outstanding past 90 days from the date of service are typically categorized as aged AR requiring specialized attention.

22. Why do accounts slip past 90 days?

Common causes include unresponsive insurance carriers, missing medical records, untimely filing deadlines, and internal billing oversight.

23. How do you tackle technical denials?

We review the root cause of the rejection, correct formatting or coding errors, and re-file or appeal with supporting documentation.

24. Do you manage direct phone escalations with payers?

Yes, we place systematic follow-up calls and navigate payer portals to track down stuck claims.

25. What is the typical timeframe for recovering aged claims?

While results vary by payer, accounts typically progress through active resolution cycles within 30 to 60 days.

26. Can you prevent future claims from aging?

Yes, through our root-cause audits, we identify recurring front-end errors to prevent future denials.

27. Do you handle patient-responsibility balances?

Our core focus is insurance AR recovery, but statements and workflows can be structured per client preference.

28. How do you manage timely filing deadlines?

We prioritize accounts approaching critical filing cutoffs to ensure appeal rights are preserved.

29. Do you handle secondary insurance follow-ups?

Yes, we ensure secondary claims are correctly triggered and processed once primary payments post.

30. What metrics do you track during recovery?

We track recovery yield percentages, days in AR, and primary denial categories.

4. Claims Auditing & Clinical Records

31. What is involved in a surgical claims audit?

We conduct deep-dive root-cause analysis of lengthy hospital operative notes, physician charts, and billing sheets.

32. Why are hospital notes difficult to review?

They often span 10 to 20 pages of dense clinical documentation where specific coding details are buried.

33. How does root-cause analysis help billing?

It pinpoints exactly why a claim was underpaid or rejected, allowing for precise corrective action.

34. Do you verify CPT and ICD-10 coding accuracy?

Yes, we cross-reference clinical documentation to ensure codes accurately reflect the services rendered.

35. Can auditing protect against external compliance audits?

36. Do you review modifier usage on surgical claims?

37. How long does a detailed record audit take?

Duration depends on record volume, but our structured review process is optimized for efficiency.

38. Do you provide written audit summary reports?

Yes, every audit includes clear findings and actionable recommendations for your billing department.

39. Are complex multi-procedure claims supported?

We specialize in untangling multi-code operative reports and unbundled billing scenarios.

40. How do audits improve cash flow?

By catching documentation flaws before submission or successfully appealing erroneous down-coding.
